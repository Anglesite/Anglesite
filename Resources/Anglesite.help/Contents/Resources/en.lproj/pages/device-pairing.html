iPhone/iPad pairing

Settings ▸ iPhone/iPad pairs a companion Anglesite app on your phone or tablet with this Mac.

Pairing a device

Open Settings ▸ iPhone/iPad to see a QR code carrying this Mac's device ID and public key. Scan it from the Anglesite app on your iPhone or iPad to pair it. Paired devices are listed below the code, each with a Revoke button to remove it.

This is early: device pairing is part of Anglesite's cross-device "Anywhere" runtime, which is still under active development. Nothing leaves your iCloud account as part of pairing.
