Agent readiness

Agent Readiness checks how well your published site works with AI crawlers and agents — separate from the deploy-readiness dot, which checks whether your site is safe to publish.

Running a check

Choose Website ▸ Agent Readiness…. Your site needs to already be published, since the check scans the live, deployed site rather than your local files.

What it checks

The scan groups its checks into four categories — Discoverability, Content, Bot Access Control, and Capabilities — covering things like whether you have a robots.txt and sitemap, whether your pages offer content in forms AI agents can read, and whether you allow or block AI crawlers on purpose rather than by accident. It reports an overall tier (for example, "Bot-Aware") alongside how many checks passed.

Some checks describe capabilities Anglesite doesn't offer yet; those show as failing with an explanation rather than being hidden, so you know what's not available versus what's misconfigured.

Requirements

Running a scan calls a Cloudflare API, so it needs a Cloudflare account connected — see Accounts and setup.
