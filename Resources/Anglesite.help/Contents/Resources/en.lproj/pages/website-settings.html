Website settings

Website settings hold the site-wide configuration for your site — separate from the app's own Settings window.

Opening Website settings

Choose Website ▸ Website Settings… (⇧⌘,), or click the Website row at the top of the Navigator sidebar.

What's in each tab

  • Website — your site's title, language, and icons.
  • Analytics — analytics provider setup and UTM codes.
  • Redirects — URL redirects for your site.
  • Licensing — the license your content is published under.
  • Email Security — MTA-STS email security policy.
  • Security Reports — where security researchers can report issues with your site.
  • Social — linked social accounts, such as Bluesky.
  • Workers — Cloudflare Worker configuration for your site.
